## Potential Reviewer Concerns & Responses

### Concern 1: "Why no proteomics validation?"

**Response**:
"While proteomics data would complement our findings, the metabolomics data alone provide robust evidence for pathway activation through: (1) significant enrichment analysis, (2) large effect sizes, (3) cross-database validation, and (4) mechanistically coherent conjugation patterns. The differential accumulation of conjugates vs. aglycones provides metabolite-level evidence of flux channeling that is independent of enzyme measurements."

### Concern 2: "Is pathway enrichment with P=0.030 sufficiently strong?"

**Response**:
"We acknowledge the modest P-value and address multiple testing considerations transparently (Section 2.7, Supplementary Discussion S2). However, the finding is supported by: (1) large effect size (OR=10.43), (2) cross-database biological concordance (PlantCyc independently ranks isoflavonoid biosynthesis highest), (3) massive metabolite fold changes (>4000×), and (4) mechanistic coherence. This converging evidence strengthens confidence beyond the nominal P-value."

### Concern 3: "Limited metabolite coverage (79 metabolites)?"

**Response**:
"While targeted metabolomics would provide deeper pathway coverage, our untargeted LC-MS/MS approach captures major metabolic shifts across metabolite classes. The 79 quantified metabolites include key isoflavonoid pathway members and provide sufficient power to detect large-magnitude pathway activation. We explicitly discuss missing metabolites (e.g., genistein) as a limitation (Section 2.7)."

### Concern 4: "Why is database mapping so low (36.7%)?"

**Response**:
"This is not a methodological failure, but rather reveals the current state of database coverage for plant specialized metabolism. Most unmapped metabolites are legume-specific conjugates (malonyl/acetyl derivatives) absent from generalist databases like KEGG. Critically, pathway enrichment was significant using only basal metabolites, demonstrating robustness. We provide detailed analysis of database coverage as a contribution highlighting annotation needs (Section 2.6, Supplementary Discussion S1)."
